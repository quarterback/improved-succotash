DeepSWE — Capability Benchmark

What agentic software work actually costs per solved task

Occupant prices AI work ($CPI), measures its volume ($AIU), and gauges its labor pressure ($LDI). Capability is the missing axis: a cheap model that fails often can cost more per solved task than an expensive one that usually succeeds. The DeepSWE leaderboard runs frontier coding agents against original, behavior-verified tasks; we re-derive it into the same quality-adjusted frame as the indices.

Reading it. Pass@1 is the share of tasks solved on a single attempt; the ± figure is the reported confidence interval, and adjacent models often overlap. Cost / solved divides a run's average cost by its pass rate, so it penalizes models that are cheap per run but frequently fail — the same quality-adjusted logic the Market Intel page applies to token pricing. Tasks are newly authored rather than scraped, so they can't leak into training data, and verifiers grade actual software behavior, not implementation specifics.
